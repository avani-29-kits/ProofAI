How it works

From question to proof, in four steps.

ProofAI doesn't just write an answer. It turns your question into code, runs it on your data, and checks the result before showing it.

01

Understand

The agent reads your question and finds the relevant tables, columns and date ranges.

02

Generate

It writes Python/Pandas code based on the real structure of your files.

03

Execute

The code actually runs against your uploaded data in a sandbox.

04

Verify

The result is re-run and checked, then returned with its proof.
